How affiliate links are handled

If a recommendation page contains an approved affiliate link, SBRP will identify it near the link. A qualifying purchase may provide compensation to SBRP at no additional cost to the purchaser, subject to the applicable program terms.

Retailer participation, affiliate approval, tracking links, prices, product availability, images, and attribution are separate matters.
