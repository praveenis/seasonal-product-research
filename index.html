Seasonal Product Discovery Engine

Data-driven product research for e-commerce, dropshipping, and print-on-demand businesses.

Independent Research Project

About the Project

Seasonal Product Discovery Engine is a Python-based application under development to identify potential e-commerce product opportunities using market trends, search demand, seasonality, and estimated profitability.

The project focuses on the United States market and supports independent product research and evaluation.

Planned Features

Market Trend Analysis

Research emerging consumer demand and seasonal product trends.

Keyword Research

Analyze keyword demand and historical search volume.

Profitability Estimation

Estimate product margins, shipping costs, and potential profit.

Product Scoring

Rank product opportunities using demand, seasonality, and margins.

Google Ads API - Intended Usage

The project intends to use the Google Ads API, subject to approval and applicable access permissions, to research keyword ideas and historical search demand.

The information will support internal product discovery and market research. The application is not intended to manage advertising campaigns for third-party clients.

Technology Stack

Python, Pandas, SQLite, Streamlit, and supported third-party APIs.

Project Status

Currently under development. Features and integrations may change.
