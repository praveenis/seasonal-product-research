About SBRP

Research before recommendations.

Seasonal Product Research (SBRP) is a research project for exploring seasonal product ideas with clear source and evidence boundaries.

How we approach research

Useful product research distinguishes observed data from estimates and unknowns. Search interest is not sales. Listing counts are not purchases. A product idea is not a verified retailer offer.

The public catalog currently contains five illustrative demos. They do not represent real listings, live market findings, product testing, or recommendations. Prices and availability are unavailable where no verified data exists.

Our standards

  • Keep sources and data status visible.
  • Use specific product identifiers when matching retailer offers.
  • Do not invent prices, availability, reviews, or personal testing.
  • Require editorial review before publishing generated content.
