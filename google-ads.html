Google Ads, used for research — not guesswork.

SBRP uses the Google Ads Keyword Planning API to discover product-shaped keywords and read historical search estimates in the U.S. market.

What it contributes

A demand signal with guardrails.

Search interest is useful context. It is not a sales forecast, proof of purchases, or a substitute for supplier and marketplace validation.

⌁

Keyword discovery

Begin with category seeds, then surface relevant product phrases without manually maintaining a product list.

◷

Monthly history

Use available monthly search estimates to identify patterns across at least twelve distinct months.

◎

U.S. English scope

Target the United States and English language settings for a focused starting point.

Access requirements

Built to respect the source.

Live keyword research requires an approved Google Ads API setup and an account with the right access level. No data is silently replaced with synthetic results when a live request fails.

01

Google Cloud project

Enable the Google Ads API and associate the OAuth application with the project used for requests.

02

OAuth credentials

Use a Google Ads OAuth client, refresh token, developer token, and an accessible customer account.

03

Production access

Keyword planning for production accounts requires the access level approved for that method, such as Basic or Standard.

Important: Google Ads search estimates are not sales. Supplier costs, shipping, inventory, and fulfillment still require separate verification.
