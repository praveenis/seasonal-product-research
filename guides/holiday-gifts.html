Buying guide · Holiday gifts

Make the gift fit the person.

A useful gift starts with what the recipient needs, likes, can use, and has room to store.

Begin with the recipient

Consider interests, existing equipment, accessibility, preferences, and any constraints the person has shared. Avoid using stereotypes as a substitute for knowing the recipient.

Check fit, compatibility, and contents

For apparel, accessories, electronics, and home goods, compare exact dimensions, model compatibility, materials, and included items. Personalization options and spelling requirements deserve a careful final check.

Plan around delivery and returns

Review current delivery estimates and order cutoffs at the seller. Check return or exchange conditions, gift receipt availability, and whether a personalized item is returnable. Do not rely on last year’s dates.

Compare full value

Include required accessories, batteries, subscriptions, shipping, and maintenance when they apply. A sale badge or seasonal theme alone does not show that an item is a good fit.

Editorial note: this guide is general research advice, not a tested gift list or endorsement.
