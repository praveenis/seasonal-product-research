Privacy Policy

How Seasonal Business Research Platform handles website, research, and Google Ads integration data.

Last updated: November 5, 2026

SSeasonal Business Research PlatformSBRP · sbrp.app

At a glance

We collect only what is needed to operate and improve SBRP, respond to support requests, and run authorized product research.

privacy@sbrp.app

Seasonal Business Research Platform (“SBRP,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) provides product research tools focused on seasonal demand, keyword trends, and potential e-commerce opportunities. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you visit sbrp.app or use an SBRP service.

1. Information we collect

We may collect:

  • Information you send to us, such as your name, email address, and the contents of a support or privacy request.
  • Research inputs and outputs, such as product categories, keywords, search estimates, rankings, and notes, when you use an SBRP research service.
  • Technical information needed to deliver and secure the website, such as request timestamps, browser type, approximate location derived from an IP address, and security logs supplied by our hosting provider.
  • Google Ads account identifiers and keyword-planning data obtained through an authorized Google Ads integration operated by SBRP.

2. How we use information

We use information to operate SBRP, provide product research results, evaluate seasonal demand, troubleshoot errors, maintain security, prevent abuse, respond to requests, and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell personal information or use Google Ads data for unrelated advertising targeting.

3. Google Ads data

SBRP may use the Google Ads API to retrieve keyword ideas and historical keyword-planning estimates for accounts that SBRP is authorized to access. The current research workflow is operator-managed: it uses authorized application credentials and does not ask visitors to submit Google passwords.

Google Ads data is used to support SBRP’s product-research features and may be combined with other research inputs to calculate seasonal indicators or opportunity scores. We do not sell Google Ads data, use it to build advertising audiences, or transfer it to third parties except as needed to provide the service, protect SBRP, comply with law, or as otherwise disclosed here.

Google’s own terms and policies also apply to Google data, including the Google API Services User Data Policy. If SBRP later adds an end-user Google authorization flow, this policy and the in-product authorization notice will be updated before that feature is used.

4. Sharing and service providers

We may share information with service providers that help host, secure, operate, or support SBRP. The public website is hosted through GitHub Pages. Google provides the Google Ads API and related authentication services. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a business transfer.

5. Storage, security, and retention

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect information. OAuth credentials and API secrets are intended to be stored outside public website files and are restricted to authorized operators.

Support requests are retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond and maintain support records. Research results and technical logs are retained only for as long as needed for the research service, troubleshooting, security, and legal obligations, and are then deleted or de-identified according to the service’s operational practices.

6. Cookies and analytics

The current public SBRP website does not use analytics cookies, advertising pixels, or behavioral tracking cookies. The site may use strictly necessary technical features provided by its hosting service to deliver pages and protect the service. If analytics or advertising tools are added, this policy will be updated before they are used.

7. Your choices and deletion requests

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with you by emailing privacy@sbrp.app. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request and may retain information where required by law or necessary to protect the service.

8. Children’s privacy

SBRP is intended for adults conducting business or product research and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as SBRP, its integrations, or its data practices change. The current version will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

10. Contact us

For privacy questions, data requests, or concerns about this policy, contact privacy@sbrp.app.
