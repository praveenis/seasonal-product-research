A gift for two

Thanksgiving gifts for your spouse

A shared plan, an easy game night, or a personal note can make a thoughtful gift without adding more work to a full holiday.

Choose a gesture that matches how the two of you like to spend time. These Target listings are examples checked November 9, 2026; ratings and review counts are retailer-displayed customer feedback, not independent testing.

Personal gifts without a shopping list

  • Write a note about something you appreciated this year.
  • Plan a low-cost date for after the holiday and take care of the arrangements.
  • Print a favorite photo, make a playlist, or recreate a meal tied to a shared memory.
  • Take a holiday task off your spouse’s plate as part of the gift.
