Make the cooking plan manageable

  • Choose a small number of dishes and check that oven, stovetop, and refrigerator space will be available.
  • Read the full recipe before shopping; note serving size, equipment, timing, and any make-ahead steps.
  • Ask guests about allergies and dietary needs, and check ingredient labels for packaged foods.
  • Keep raw foods separate from ready-to-eat foods and use a food thermometer for meat and poultry.

For food-safety details, use FoodSafety.gov's safe minimum internal temperature chart and the USDA's leftovers and food-safety guidance. Follow current official recommendations for cooking, cooling, storage, and reheating.
