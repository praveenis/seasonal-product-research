Thanksgiving / Games and activities

Make it easy to join

Family games and activities for Thanksgiving

A good gathering activity gives people an easy way to join and an easy way to step away. These ideas need little preparation and no special purchase.

Table memory round

Group: 3 or more. Materials: none. Setup: none. Time: about 5-15 minutes, depending on the group.

Invite each person to name one small thing they noticed or enjoyed this year. Anyone may pass, draw, write, or ask someone else to read their answer. Keep it light; no one needs to explain a difficult year.

Picture and guess

Group: 3 or more. Materials: scrap paper and pencils. Setup: about 2 minutes. Time: about 10-20 minutes.

One player draws a familiar object, place, or food while others guess. Skip the timer for younger players or anyone who prefers more time. A partner can draw or describe for someone who prefers another way to participate.

Holiday category circle

Group: 4 or more. Materials: none. Setup: none. Time: about 5-10 minutes.

Choose a friendly category: a favorite soup, something red, or a place to visit. Go around naming one answer each. Repeats are fine, and people may pass. Skip elimination rules so latecomers can join.

A quiet creative option

Set out blank cards and colored pencils for a drawing, a note, or a recipe memory. There is no score, purchase, or group performance required.

Keep it comfortable

  • Offer seated participation, large-print prompts, and extra thinking time.
  • Let people pass without explanation, and provide a quieter alternative.
  • Ask before inviting anyone into a game; adapt materials to the people playing.

Group sizes and durations are planning estimates, not measured results. These are original activity suggestions; no commercial game ratings or popularity claims are used.
