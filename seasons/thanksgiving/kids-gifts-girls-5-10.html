Choose by the child, not the stereotype

  • Ask what she is into right now: animals, art, puzzles, sports, building, stories, or something else.
  • Consider patience, fine-motor comfort, and whether she prefers to create alone or together.
  • Check small parts, cleanup, storage, age guidance, and caregiver preferences before buying.
  • Offer to work on the project together; let her decide how to use or display what she makes.
