A thoughtful thank-you

Thanksgiving host gift ideas

A small host gift can say thank you without giving the host another task. Consider something shareable, easy to set aside, and suited to the people at the table.

Keep the gesture easy

  • Ask about food allergies or dietary preferences if you are unsure.
  • Choose a gift that does not require the host to serve it immediately.
  • A handwritten thank-you note or an offer to help clean up can be just as welcome.
  • Check delivery dates and return terms if ordering online.
