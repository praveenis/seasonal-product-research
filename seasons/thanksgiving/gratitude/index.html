Thanksgiving / Prayers and gratitude

A moment, in your own way

Share thanks in a way that feels right

A shared pause can be religious, secular, spoken, written, or quiet. Let guests know that joining in is optional and passing is welcome.

Christian prayer (original wording)

God of love, thank you for this meal, for the hands that prepared it, and for the people gathered here. Help us share what we have with care and welcome one another with kindness. Amen.

Gratitude reflection (original, non-liturgical)

With gratitude for the earth's gifts and for the work that brings food to our table, may this meal strengthen our care for one another and for our neighbors. May we share with generosity.

This is an original reflection, not a traditional blessing or substitute for a community's practice. Guests who prefer a traditional prayer can use the wording and custom of their own community.

A secular table pause

Take one quiet breath. If you'd like, name one small thing from this week that you are glad you noticed, received, or shared. You can also pass, write it down, or simply listen.

A conversation prompt

"What is one kindness, given or received, that you want to carry into the next week?" No explanation is required.

The prayers and prompts on this page are original wording for reflection. They are not presented as traditional liturgy. Participation is always optional.
