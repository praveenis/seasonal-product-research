Family time

Thanksgiving gifts from grandparents to grandkids

A game, a small art project, or a coloring kit can give grandparents and grandkids an easy way to spend time together during the visit.

Google Trends lists coloring pages, games, scavenger hunts, and read-alouds among recent U.S. Thanksgiving activity searches. That gives a seasonal clue for activity-focused gifts, but does not show exact search volumes, purchases, or what grandparents are buying. See the Google Trends activity page.

Make the gift fit the visit

  • Ask the child's parent about current interests, age, allergies or sensitivities, and household rules.
  • A game can be ready to play; a craft may need setup, space, and cleanup.
  • Offer to play or make something together—the time can be part of the gift.
  • Follow the product’s age guidance and supervise children when needed.
