Quick guide by age

  • Around 5–7: Look for clear steps and plan to join in or help.
  • Around 8–10: A longer building or science activity may fit a child who likes instructions and experimenting.
  • Before buying: Ask the child or caregiver about interests, existing toys, sensitivities, and household rules.
